Common questions

How long does it take?

About 7 days from when I have your text and photos. A bigger site or a custom tool can take longer. If it will, I'll tell you the timeline before we start.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, the services you offer, the areas you cover, rough prices if you want them shown, and photos of your work. Phone photos are fine. If you don't have a logo, I'll set your name in a clean text style. Not sure what to write? Tell me about your business on WhatsApp and I'll help you with the wording.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the website and all its files are yours. I'll hand them over whenever you ask, and you're free to work with someone else later.

What does free hosting mean?

Hosting is the service that keeps your website online. I put your site on reliable hosting that has no monthly fee, so you don't pay anything to keep it running. The only yearly cost you might choose to add is your own domain name (see below).

What if I need changes later?

You have two options. The Care Plan, from S$50 a month, covers text changes, new photos, price updates and small fixes whenever you need them. Or just WhatsApp me when something comes up, and I'll quote you for that one change before doing it.

How do I pay?

By PayNow. After you've seen and approved your free design preview, you pay 50% to start the build. You pay the other 50% when you're happy with the finished site. There are no hidden fees.

Can you do something that isn't listed?

Often, yes. Tell me what you need on WhatsApp. If it's something I can build well, I'll give you a price. If it isn't, I'll say so and suggest another way.

Do I need a domain name?

You don't need one to go live. Your site can start on a free web address. A domain of your own, like yourbusiness.sg, looks more professional and usually costs around S$20 to S$60 a year depending on the ending. You buy it in your own name, so it always belongs to you. I'll help you choose one and connect it.
