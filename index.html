    Questions

    What do I need to give you?

    Your business name, services, areas, rough prices and phone photos of your work. No logo? I'll set your name in clean text. Not sure what to write? I'll help on WhatsApp. Once I have it, you're live in 5 working days.

    How do I pay?

    By PayNow. 50% after you approve your free preview, 50% when you're happy with the finished site. No hidden fees.

    Do I own the website?

    Yes. After the final payment the site and all its files are yours, and you can work with someone else later.

    What's free, and what about hosting and a domain?

    You own your site and can host it free with my setup guide. Managed hosting is optional: Hosting is S$50 a month (hosting, SSL and basic backups, no edits included), and Full care is S$150 a month (hosting, updates, support and 2 edits a month, all handled by me). A domain of your own, like yourbusiness.sg, is optional: about S$20 to S$60 a year, bought in your name. I'll help you choose and connect it.

    What if I need changes later?

    Full care (S$150 a month) includes 2 edits a month: text, photo or price changes. On either plan, extra edits are S$50 each, and the Hosting plan (S$50 a month) includes no edits. Each automation (like a chatbot or WhatsApp auto-reply) adds S$50 a month. Or WhatsApp me and I'll quote that one change before doing it.

    How does refer a friend work?

    Refer a friend who buys a website package: they get S$100 off, and I send you S$100 by PayNow after they pay in full. Your friend gives me your name before paying the deposit. It stacks with any sale or code. It covers website packages only, not monthly fees. One referral discount per order, no limit on how many friends you refer, and you can't refer your own business.
