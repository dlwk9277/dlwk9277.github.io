Connect your domain to your site

Six steps across Netlify, Namecheap and Google Search Console. About 20 minutes of clicking, then a wait for DNS. Below, YOURDOMAIN.com is your domain and YOURSITE is your Netlify site name.

Menu names change now and then. Can't find a button? WhatsApp me and I'll point you to it. Never send your passwords to anyone, including me.

  1. In Netlify, add your domain

    Netlify is where your site lives. Tell it which domain you own.

    • Log in to app.netlify.com and open your site.
    • Go to Domain management (under Site configuration).
    • Click Add a domain, type YOURDOMAIN.com, then click Verify and Add domain.
    • If Netlify offers its own DNS, skip it. We keep DNS at Namecheap, so the next steps set it up there.

  2. In Namecheap, open Advanced DNS

    This is the page where you point the domain at your site.

    • Log in to namecheap.com and click Domain List.
    • Click Manage next to your domain.
    • On the Domain tab, check that Nameservers says Namecheap BasicDNS.
    • Open the Advanced DNS tab.

  3. Delete the parking records

    A new domain comes with two records that show a Namecheap parking page. They block your site.

    • Find the CNAME record with Host www and Value parkingpage.namecheap.com.. Click the bin icon.
    • Find the URL Redirect Record with Host @. Delete it too.
    • Also delete any AAAA (IPv6) record, and any other A, CNAME or redirect record on @ or www. Netlify does not support IPv6, so old records clash.
    • Leave any other records alone, especially email (MX) records.

  4. Add the two records

    Click Add New Record twice and fill in these. Use the values Netlify shows you if they differ from mine.

    • A Record: Host @, Value 75.2.60.5, TTL Automatic.
    • CNAME Record: Host www, Value YOURSITE.netlify.app, TTL Automatic.
    • Click the green tick on each row to save it.

  5. Back in Netlify, turn on HTTPS

    HTTPS is the padlock. Customers and Google both expect it.

    • Wait about 30 minutes. Sometimes DNS takes a few hours, and up to 48 at most.
    • In Domain management, click Verify DNS configuration next to your domain.
    • Scroll to HTTPS and click Provision certificate.
    • In Domain management, make www.YOURDOMAIN.com your primary domain. Netlify recommends this when DNS stays with another provider such as Namecheap.
    • Open https://YOURDOMAIN.com on your phone. You should see your site and a padlock.

  6. Add the site to Google Search Console

    This tells Google your site exists, so customers can find it when they search.

    • Go to search.google.com/search-console and sign in with your Google account.
    • Click Add property, choose Domain, type YOURDOMAIN.com and click Continue.
    • Google shows a TXT record. Copy it.
    • In Namecheap Advanced DNS, click Add New Record: TXT Record, Host @, Value the text you copied. Save it.
    • Back in Search Console, click Verify. If it fails, wait 10 minutes and try again. Keep the TXT record afterwards: if you delete it, Google can un-verify you.
    • Open Sitemaps, type sitemap.xml and click Submit.
