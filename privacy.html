Privacy notice

This page explains, in plain English, what this website collects and why. Last updated: November 2026.

Meta Pixel cookies

This site may use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It sets cookies that help me see whether my Instagram and Facebook ads lead people to this site and to contact me. For example, it records that someone visited the page or tapped a WhatsApp button.

I use this only to measure and improve my ads. I don't see your name or personal details from it. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.

You can limit this tracking by:

  • adjusting your ad preferences in your Meta Accounts Center
  • blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings

WhatsApp messages

When you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a message ready for you to send. Nothing is sent until you press send. If you message me, I use your name, number and message only to reply to your enquiry and to work with you on your project. I don't sell or share your details, and I don't add you to marketing lists.

Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most websites, the host may record basic technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the page. Fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact Google to load them.
