    Questions

    How much is a website for an aircon company?

    S$500 to S$4,400, free to self-host with my setup guide, or I can host it for you. The price depends on the pages and extras you pick: use the calculator above. A domain of your own is optional, about S$20 to S$60 a year.

    Can customers see my servicing prices?

    Yes. I put your prices on the page by number of units, and can add a calculator so a customer gets a total before messaging. You decide what to show; anything you would rather not publish can say "WhatsApp for a quote".

    I only have phone photos. Is that enough?

    Yes. Photos from your phone work well: a dirty coil and a clean one, the unit opened up. If you have none yet, I build the site without them and you add photos later. I do not use stock photos to pretend they are your jobs.

    Can the site work in Chinese too?

    Yes. A switch at the top changes the whole site between English and Chinese. Have a Chinese speaker check the wording before you share it.

    How do I pay, and who owns the site?

    PayNow: 50% after you approve your free preview, 50% when you are happy with the finished site. After the final payment the site and all its files are yours.

    How does refer a friend work?

    Refer a friend who buys a website package: they get S$100 off, and I send you S$100 by PayNow after they pay in full. Your friend gives me your name before paying the deposit. It stacks with any sale. It covers website packages only, not monthly fees. One referral discount per order, no limit on how many friends you refer, and you can't refer your own business.
